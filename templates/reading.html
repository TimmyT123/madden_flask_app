Attention training

If your mind wanders, it's fine to return to the sentence and continue. Don't force yourself to eliminate rereading. The goal is to notice distraction sooner and follow the meaning of the passage.

Rate your focus after each reading. 1 = frequently distracted; 5 = fully engaged. You can change this before submitting the questions.

Practice routine (10–15 minutes)

Read one passage normally, answer the questions, then reflect on where your attention went. On another day, try reading a little quicker while keeping your comprehension steady.
