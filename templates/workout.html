Training instructions

How to use this coach

Warm up 5–10 minutes. For each exercise, do the listed working sets and reps. Leave about 1–3 reps in reserve rather than reaching exhaustion every set. Rest 90–120 seconds between most sets, and 2–3 minutes after demanding lifts if needed.

Progress rule: When you complete all sets at the top of the rep range with controlled technique, increase the weight a small amount next time. At first, aim for the lower number of sets if the workout is unfamiliar.

Home equipment: Bedroom dumbbells and the Bowflex are alternatives or a convenient way to finish an exercise you missed. Don't repeat an entire gym session at home. On leg day, the last two arm exercises can be done at home after the gym, not before.

Safety: Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. If you have ongoing shoulder, elbow, knee, back, or heart-related limitations, adapt the exercises with a qualified professional.
