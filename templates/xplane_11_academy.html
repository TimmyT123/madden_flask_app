      📡 Sample radio calls

      Speak these aloud. These are learning examples, not actual clearances or verified current frequencies; use the callsign and instructions given in your simulation.

      Ground: taxi request

      “Ground, Cessna November One Two Three Alpha Bravo, at general aviation parking, ready to taxi, VFR departure.”

      Tower: departure

      “Tower, Cessna One Two Three Alpha Bravo, holding short runway two five, ready for departure.”

      Approach: position report

      “Approach, Cessna One Two Three Alpha Bravo, five miles east, three thousand five hundred, inbound for landing.”

      Readback practice

      “Runway two five, cleared to land, Cessna One Two Three Alpha Bravo.”

      Real-world ATC phraseology varies by region, airspace and clearance. X-Plane 11's built-in ATC does not simulate every real-world interaction.
