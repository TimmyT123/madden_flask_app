Recognition checks whether the words resemble the target; it cannot reliably grade accent or articulation. Listen to your pronunciation and try again.

Pronunciation tips

j — a breathy “h”: jamón
ñ — “ny”: niño
ll / y — often like English “y” or “j”, depending on dialect
r / rr — a quick tap vs. trill
vowels — a, e, i, o, u stay fairly consistent

For microphone practice, try Chrome or Edge over HTTPS (or localhost), allow microphone permission, and be aware that recognition may use an online service.
