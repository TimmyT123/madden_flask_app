Monday • October 12

Plantation History Day
Reserved • 9:30 AM

Whitney Plantation

🚙 Leave New Orleans around 8:15 AM
🎧 Self-guided audio tour

Whitney centers the lives and experiences of enslaved people, making this the more serious and history-focused plantation visit.

✓ Reservation confirmed
Why we’re doing this one
  • Focuses on slavery and the people who lived and worked on the plantation.
  • Different experience from Oak Alley, so the two visits complement each other.
  • Plan roughly two hours before lunch.

The iconic plantation setting: the famous oak-lined approach, historic house, grounds, sugarcane history, and exhibits.

Important timing
  • Oak Alley requires arrival at least 30 minutes before the ticket time.

  • We’ll have lunch between Whitney and Oak Alley.
