Which ways make the most sense for you?

START HERE · LOW FINANCIAL RISK

Deal scout / analyst

Practice comps, repair estimates, rent research and maximum-offer calculations. You can learn without buying anything. Getting paid for sourcing or brokering deals may trigger licensing requirements: check before offering services.

Best first skill
IF PROPERTY ALLOWS

Use property you already own

Evaluate a room, casita, or permitted ADU, factoring in zoning, HOA, privacy, insurance, utilities and tax implications. You might not need another mortgage.

Low new acquisition cost
LONG-TERM PATH

Buy-and-hold rental

Seek positive cash flow after vacancy, management, repairs and capital expenses. Prefer cash flow over bets on appreciation.

Capital intensive
HANDS-ON, HIGHER RISK

Fix and flip

Potentially suits DIY interests, but contracting, permits, resale costs, carrying costs, and schedule overruns make mistakes expensive.

LOW-EFFORT OPTION

Publicly traded REITs

Real estate exposure without direct tenants or renovation. Market prices, dividends and principal can fluctuate. Diversification matters.

LEARN FIRST

Wholesaling contracts

May require less purchase capital, but is not risk-free or automatically license-free. Arizona has specific written disclosure requirements before binding agreements.

Retirement guardrail: keep your emergency reserve separate; avoid relying on projected appreciation or using home equity to fund your first speculative deal. These are learning tools, not personal investment, legal, or tax advice.
