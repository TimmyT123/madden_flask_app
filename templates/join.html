Why Join WURD?

  • Active Madden franchise league
  • Organized Discord communication
  • Regular advance schedule
  • League website with stats, standings, schedules, and recaps
  • Commissioners who help manage games, rules, and activity
  • Queue system for new recruits

What We Look For

  • Reliable users who communicate
  • Players who can schedule games respectfully
  • Users who follow league rules
  • Stable internet connection
  • No quitting, drama, or disrespect toward commissioners

How It Works

1. Join Discord

Enter the WURD recruit area with limited Recruit access.

2. Read Rules

Review the league rules and expectations before applying.

3. Complete App

The WURD bot will send you the recruit application link.

4. Get Reviewed

Commissioners review your application before moving you into Queue.

League Rules Snapshot

  • Communicate with your opponent
  • Respect commissioners and final rulings
  • Play fair and avoid cheese gameplay
  • Do not quit user vs user games
  • Stay active and respond when your game channel is created
