Three rules before spending a dollar

1. Protect your runway

Separate retirement/emergency funds from the money you can truly risk. Model six bad months, not just a good year.

2. Pay for profit, not hype

Revenue, social followers, and seller claims do not equal cash you can take home.

3. Buy a job or an asset?

Estimate the hours you must personally work and the cost of hiring someone to replace you.

Franchising

FTC: consumer guide to buying a franchiseFederal franchise disclosure rule

Review all 23 FDD items. The FTC rule generally requires delivery at least 14 calendar days before signing or paying the franchisor or affiliate; consult qualified counsel on your situation.

Acquisitions & loans

SBA 7(a) programSBA: buy a business or franchise

Ask lenders about eligibility, cash injection, collateral, guarantees and debt service. Eligibility and approval are not automatic.

Arizona setup

Arizona Corporation Commission: 10 stepsArizona Business One Stop

Check entity formation, local permits, Arizona transaction privilege tax obligations, insurance, and relevant professional licenses.

Before buying: Have an acquisition attorney and CPA review tax returns, bank statements, financials, payroll, debt, liens, leases, contracts, customer concentration, legal claims, equipment condition, working capital, and seller representations. For a franchise, add FDD Items 5–7, 19–21, unit openings/closures, territory, renewals, personal guarantees, approved suppliers, and conversations with current/former franchisees.
