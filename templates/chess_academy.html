Weekly improvement plan

At around 1700, prioritize calculation discipline and analyzing your own serious games. Follow this rotation; check sessions off as you complete them. Reset each week when you want a fresh cycle.

Your calculation routine (use in every position)

1. Identify threats and king safety. 2. List 2–3 candidate moves. 3. Calculate checks, captures and forcing threats for both sides. 4. Evaluate the final position, not just the first attractive move. 5. Do one blunder check before committing.

How to review one of your own games

First annotate without an engine: identify the critical moment, your candidates, and what you missed. Only then use an engine or stronger player's analysis. Record the cause: calculation, visualization, positional judgment, time pressure, opening knowledge or endgame technique.

A strong way to use famous games

Hide the next move at 3–5 turning points, spend 3–7 minutes calculating, then compare your choice with the game and with analysis. Don't assume every unusual move is a blunder or every sacrifice is objectively winning. Separate engine evaluation, human practicality, and long-term strategy.
